Leagues

The list comes from the Currency Exchange data. Add a league it does not show yet, such as a new or private league, or set which league's rules a private league follows.

The rules decide league mechanics such as Runic Alloys and which recipes apply. A league without exchange data uses the prices of the league whose rules it follows, and the page says so; prices you set in the exchange window still win. The trade link and the prices you log use the name you enter. Saved in this browser only.

Paste item

Hover the item in game, press Ctrl Alt C, then press Ctrl V here. The item loads as soon as it is pasted. A screenshot of the tooltip (with Alt held) works too: paste it here, choose the file, or drop it anywhere on the page.
