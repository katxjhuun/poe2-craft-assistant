# What crafters do in 0.5.5 (Forbidden Rites): research log, 7 Oct 2026

Why: the player's verdict on 7 Oct 2026 was that the assistant "only shows fixed paths" and misses the easier and
cheaper ways they know. This log collects what people who craft and publish actually do, with dates, so the planner can
be rebuilt on it. Dates matter: 0.5.0 came out on 29 May 2026, 0.5.5 on 4 Sept 2026; anything older is kept only where
the mechanic still exists.

How it was read: the index of 0.5.5 crafting videos at poebuilds.cc/poe2/crafting (57 videos), each video's own
description (steps, chapter titles, trade search links; read from the watch page's data, no playback), and written
guides. Video transcripts could not be read: YouTube and the transcript sites answer the built-in browser with a bot
check, which was left alone. So chapter titles and descriptions are the evidence for videos; where a step list below is
only a chapter list it says so.

## The patterns (what the planner must be able to express)

P1. **Start from a bought item, not from a white base.** Nearly every profit craft starts with a trade search: a Magic
    item that already has the key mod (SaVeQ, wands: Magic, "+4 to Level of all ... Spell Skills"), a Rare with the
    key mod and open slots (SaVeQ, amulets: "+3 Level of all Spell Skills", at most 2 suffixes, up to 600 ex), a base
    with the wanted mod already fractured (Opulent Exile staff, MMOEXP jewel write-up, Diztoh Vile Robe), or an
    almost finished item with one flaw ("fixing": SaVeQ amulets up to 150 div; jewel with a bad desecration).
P2. **Fix, don't build.** Buy a near-finished item whose flaw is cheap to remove (a bad Desecrated mod: Omen of Light +
    Annulment, then bone again; one bad prefix: side-omen Chaos), sell the repaired item. Quoted: a jewel that costs
    about 240 div to craft is often fixed for 160-180 (MMOEXP write-up of a creator video, 11 July 2026).
P3. **Annul down, then Chaos spam.** With the key mod fractured, annul to one other modifier; every Chaos Orb then
    replaces exactly that modifier: a one-currency reroll. Opulent Exile (26 Sept 2026): "Annul down to 1 mod, Chaos
    spam for T1 spell damage (around 1400c, 175d)". Jewel write-up: about 1 in 73 per Chaos on an Emerald.
P4. **Fracture at 1 in 3.** Three modifiers plus a not yet revealed Desecrated one make the four that a Fracturing Orb
    needs, and the orb picks among the three ("Add to 3 mods, desecrate, fracture: 1/3 to hit", Opulent Exile; also
    the amulet write-up "three candidates, approximately one in three"). The planner knows only 1 in 4 or 1 in 5.
P5. **Desecration as a tool, not only as the last slot.** (a) reveal loop with a lich omen + Abyssal Echoes, miss ->
    Omen of Light + Annulment -> bone again (Hax jewel: about 8 tries, about 6 div each); (b) a Desecrated mod to
    block a slot while the other side is rolled (Hax ring: "Blocking the last prefix slot via desecration");
    (c) spamming desecrations without Omens of Light on amulets and rings (SaVeQ, 16 Sept 2026; method not read);
    (d) Essence of the Abyss leaves a Mark of the Abyssal Lord that the next desecration replaces (codex).
P6. **Two crafted modifiers with Astrid's Creativity.** Socket the rune, then an alloy and an essence (or two alloys)
    each with Omen of Dextral/Sinistral Crystallisation to choose the side that loses a modifier (Opulent Exile staff:
    Celestial Alloy, Astrid's Creativity 5d, Transcendent Alloy; Keyson and Lolcohol chapter "Alloys"; note.com wand
    guide, 4 July 2026; LilBotQ boots chapter "Astrid's Creativity").
P7. **Catalyst quality first, then Catalysing Exaltation.** Quality to 40% on breach rings ("Speed catalyst to 40%
    quality", Hax ring), then Omen of Catalysing Exaltation; quality must be put back before the next weighted step.
    Codex (unverified): the omen multiplies the weight about 5x at 20% and about 7.5x at 40%.
P8. **Omen of Putrefaction as a slot machine.** Buy many plain high item level bases (XTheFarmerX filter: Ancestral
    Tiara, item level 82+, not corrupted), make all modifiers Desecrated at once, sell the hits (chapters "Putrefaction
    Crafting", "Crafting Gloves With Putrefaction"; method not read in detail). The player confirmed on 25 Sept 2026
    that the omen works this way in 0.5.5; the codex's claim that it no longer does is wrong for this league.
P9. **Perfect Transmutation/Augmentation on good bases** to start with high tiers (WesDesu body armour, 2 Sept 2026),
    then Regal/essence and slams; the cheap league start version uses Greater essences (LilBotQ boots budget: Greater
    Essence -> desecrate -> Exalt slam -> reveal).
P10. **Finish with a gamble only when the item is done**: Sanctification, Vaal, Orb of Sacrifice/Architect on corrupted
    items (Mattjestic notes: "1/6 chance of bad RNG using Orb of Sacrifice"), Omen of the Blessed + Divine for
    implicits.
P11. **Batch crafts on cheap items.** Belton, "350 Divine Profit in 1 Hour Crafting 5c Items" (26 Sept 2026; method
    not read); POEGuy low investment jewellery; SaVeQ early league body armours, belts, wands; TheSaneExile
    resistance belts and rings "for half price".
P12. **Jewels**: fractured crit base -> annul + Chaos spam for the second crit mod -> Exalt + Potent Liquid Contempt
    (50/50) -> Dextral Necromancy + Preserved Cranium -> remove the allowance prefix -> Chaos spam prefixes -> Potent
    Liquid Ferocity or Melancholy (50/50) -> Divine. About 150 div per jewel (write-up of 11 July 2026). The assistant's
    five-modifier jewel route (c15) matches this.
P13. **Not gear crafting but sold as "crafts"**: Loreweave from rings (LilBotQ, 27 Sept 2026), the Taming ring (Hax),
    Genesis Tree rings and belts (Mezuman, 22 Sept 2026), +1/23% skill gems (Mattjestic). Out of the planner's scope
    except as recipes.

## What this means for the assistant

1. The planner's world starts at the pasted item and moves by one fixed rule set. Crafters choose the **entry point**
   by price (P1, P2) and shape the item so that the next random step has few outcomes (P3, P4, P5b). Neither is in the
   planner.
2. Buy prices of bases and finished items are needed for P1, P2 and for any buy-craft-sell recipe. The assistant has
   no item prices (the trade site is not queried since 7 Oct 2026). Until that changes, recipes carry the prices their
   source quoted, with the date, and the player's own numbers.
3. Open questions to settle before they enter the rules: P4 (does the Fracturing Orb never pick an unrevealed
   Desecrated modifier?), P5c (how desecrations are repeated without Omen of Light), P7 (the omen's multipliers), P8
   (the exact Putrefaction batch).
