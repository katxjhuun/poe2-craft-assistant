# Phase 2: PoE2 1.0 ahead of the patch notes (12 Nov 2026)

Roadmap phase 2, items 1-4: a second fractured modifier and the Forgemark / reforge edges in the craft network, the
`reforge` operator in the strategies, the league as a parameter in the price pipeline, and small local 1.0 scenarios.
Everything 1.0 is off unless the knowledge base has the 1.0 items or the caller asks for it (`input.v1`, `POE2_V1=1`).

## Rules first

Ledger regenerated before the work (155 rules; now 157). The 1.0 rules the work rests on were asked in one message;
the player took the working answers (12 Nov 2026). They stay `unknown` / `weak` in the ledger, with the working value
named, and live in one place, `planner.js V1_RULES`, so that the patch notes (about 4 Dec) change a value and nothing
else:

| Rule | Working answer | Parameter |
|---|---|---|
| V1_TWO_FRACTURED (weak) | up to two fractured modifiers | `maxFractured: 2` |
| how two are reached | only by one reforge with two marks: no mark on a fractured item, no Fracturing Orb on a reforged one | `markFractured: false` |
| V1_FRACTURE_ORB | four modifiers, unchanged | `orbMinMods: 4` |
| V1_FORGE_SIDE | the new modifier is of the marked one's side | `sameSide: true` (false: no reforge edge in the network) |
| V1_FORGE_POOL (new) | the item's own pool at its item level (64 or lower), usual weights | (the engine's roll) |
| V1_BLOOD_GROUPS | unknown groups: no route counts on a Bloodmark | `bloodRoutes: false` |
| V1_MIX | no Forgemark and Bloodmark reforged together | `mix: false` |
| V1_BENCH (new) | mark and reforge in one step, the bench costs nothing more | (the edge's price: the marking items) |
| V1_EXISTING | existing currencies unchanged | (patch notes) |

## What changed

- **Network** (`app/network.js`): `fh` / `fk`, a second fractured target / modifier nobody asked for (in the key only
  with 1.0). A reforge edge on Rare items of item level 64 or lower with no fractured modifier: one or two marks on
  modifiers nobody asked for (or one in a target's way, or a twin), each rolled again on its side and fractured. A
  rolled modifier that keeps a target out for good is a node of its own where the item is given up (the reader of the
  item knows a fractured modifier in any target's way with 1.0); one that stops a target of the other side is the item
  lost (a new base). `net.marksFor(step, item)` names the modifiers a step marks; `net.forge` the item and its price.
- **Play** (`scripts/selftest/network_check.js`): a reforge step is checked and played with its marks found on the item.
- **Strategies** (`app/planner.js`): `reforge` setting (in `SPACE_V1`, not in `SPACE`: without 1.0 it is never read
  and the exhaustive sweep is the same); on a goal side with no open slot, the modifiers no goal wants are marked.
- **Prices**: `app/data/leagues.json` (craft league, poe2db's league, leagues expected soon); `POE2_LEAGUE`,
  `POE2_POE2DB_LEAGUE`; `meta.json` lists Conquerors of Wraeclast and its six items with no price ("unpriced") until
  the exchange trades them; `load().unpriced` in the self-tests. No price, no step: the network leaves the Forgemark
  out today (the trap the sweeps know as missingPrices).
- **Tools**: `diag/same_cost.js` (two network files, same scenarios, cost and node count), `diag/v1_scen.js` (1.0
  scenarios at item level 64), `POE2_V1`, `V1_ILVL`, `V1_FORGE_PRICE` for every diag tool.

## What the plays found (and fixed)

1. The play checked a reforge step before its marks were named: every one was refused and a detour taken (2,892 detours
   in 2,000 crafts). Now the marks are found first.
2. A reforged modifier in the way of a target the network does not follow was read as "a fractured modifier nobody
   asked for": the route kept rolling for that target, and 46 of 1,000 crafts took an Essence of the Mind and a bone
   in turn for ever. With 1.0 the reader knows such a fractured blocker, and the edge leads to that node.
3. The edge gave roll's classes of levels as one class: nodes with no number for a class, never found by the reader
   (the same never-ending crafts). Now as the other edges do (`arrivals`).
