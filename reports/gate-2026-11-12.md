## 3. The replay gate, the known recipes, the stamp

`app/gate.js` decides each route:

- **recommend** only when the 95% interval of the played cost lies within 2% of the promise and the Wilson interval
  of the success chance lies within 0.5 points;
- **unsure** otherwise, and always when the route rests on an unknown rule;
- **refused** when the first check fails.

`scripts/gate.js` plays the list, sets each route beside the known recipes, and stamps every result with the
ledger hash, each used rule's hash and the price date. The known recipes are the crafters' standard routes and the
library's recipes of the class, simulated with 1,000 planner crafts; a recipe 5% cheaper is a defect.
`--stale` reruns only the results whose rules changed. `.github/workflows/gate.yml` runs it at 100,000 crafts.

### Blacksteel Sabatons through workflow B

`transitions.js`, 3,000 crafts: played 201.5 against 247.3 promised (x0.815). The gap is at node 463, where a Rare
with "+# to maximum Life" (the "or" slot Life / Armour / Mana) and two other prefixes takes Omen of Sinistral
Necromancy with a Preserved Rib.

- The network counts a one-in-three chance that the bone removes Life and leaves the slot empty (33.3%).
- The simulator: in 12.1% of the crafts the item still has the slot filled by an alternative that was already on it
  among the "others" (Armour 6.7%, Mana 5.4%). Only 7.3% leave the slot empty with nothing in the way, and 11.6%
  leave it with a Mana in the way.

So the network reads the other alternatives of an "or" slot as plain other modifiers. When the one it counts is
lost, it does not know that the slot is still filled, and it promises too much. The fix is in the network's
reading of an item (`nodeOf` and the "or" state). That is phase 2's file, and the brief keeps this branch to the
promise side, so it is open. Until then the gate keeps the route "unsure".

### Dueling Wand's refused bone steps

Goal: Dueling Wand set5, unwanted desecrated modifier. Its network is 268,454 nodes, solved in 99 s here. 500 crafts
played 81,289 against 85,312 promised (-4.7%, interval -12.0% to +2.5%). All ended, with no gave-up and no crafts off
the network.

The simulator refused 10 bone steps, each for "The Well of Souls would have no modifier to offer in the place of the
Mark" (claim k59, Essence of the Abyss). Run 19 had 148 of them. Run 19's counts are over up to 100,000 crafts, so
the rate today (2% of crafts) is not lower. The cause is the one the page already handles (`answer` gives the next
step the rules allow): the network's node does not know which modifiers the Well could offer on the Mark's side.
It stays open, in the network's reading of an item.

The same check found a defect in the gate: the rules of a route were collected only up to a new start, so this
wand's route was stamped without its bone and k59 rules. That is fixed: `routeActions` follows the network's start
node after a restart, and a test covers it.
